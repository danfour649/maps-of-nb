# 008 — Fredericton game boundary (derived encompassing circle)

**Publisher:** Derived here from GeoNB / GNB Environment and Local Government
**Source service:** `GeoNB_ELG_Local_Governance/MapServer/8` ("The City of Fredericton")
**Status:** Wired in 2026-11-13 — game-mesh extracts and the games' GeoNB fetches
clip to it; regenerated by `scripts/build_game_boundary.py`

## Why it exists

The legal municipal boundary is the wrong working extent for the game world:

- It **slices the Saint John River west of downtown** — the city limit crosses
  the river there, so any dataset clipped to the boundary (water, roads,
  buildings) amputates the west reach. This is the root cause of the MVTF
  "river cut off west of downtown" defect.
- Its annexation lobes and survey-straight excursions read as **choppy damage
  from the air** when the boundary is the visible edge of the detailed world.

## What it is

A circle that fully encompasses the official boundary:

- **Centre:** area centroid (shoelace) of the official polygon —
  −66.64068, 45.95877
- **Radius:** farthest official-boundary vertex from that centre
  (14.81 km) + 1.5 km margin, rounded up to the next 500 m — **16,500 m**
- **Geometry:** 180-segment polygon, committed as
  `data/game-exports/fredericton/boundary_game.geojson` and mirrored
  byte-identically into GTAfredericton as `data/boundary_game.geojson`.
  `tests/test_game_boundary.py` drift-guards it against the committed
  official boundary.

Live GeoNB contents (2026-11-13, intersects): 47,598 buildings and 5,314 NBRN
segments inside the circle, vs 30,459 / 3,651 inside the legal limit — the
margin is mostly rural York County, which the game grid already spans.

## What it is not

It is **not the legal city limit** and must never be labelled as such. The
official polygon stays in `data/game-exports/fredericton/boundary.geojson`,
keeps driving the civic extracts (parcels, PAN, addresses, HRDEM gap) and the
viewer's boundary outline, "Whole city" fit and citywide totals.
