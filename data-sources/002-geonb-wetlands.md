# 002 — GeoNB Wetlands

**Publisher:** GeoNB / NB Department of Environment and Local Government
**Service:** `GeoNB_ENV_Wetlands` (ArcGIS REST, geonb.snb.ca/arcgis)
**Status:** Wired in 2026-10-18 — viewer overlay + full-city game export

## What it is

The province's regulated wetland inventory as polygons: layer 2 "Wetland"
(all mapped wetlands) and layer 1 "PSW" (Provincially Significant
Wetlands, a protected subset). Attributes include `WETLAND_CLASS`
(marsh / bog / fen / swamp classes), `Hectares` and `STATUS`.

## Role for the game

Surface typing, not decoration: wetland polygons become soft/slow
ground in the game world, marsh visual zones, and a guide for where
ponds and marsh water belong in the terrain stage. Boundaries are
regulatory mapping — approximate by nature, which is fine for gameplay.

The downtown pilot tile has zero wetlands (it's built up); they live on
the floodplain fringe, around Killarney Lake and the city's edges —
exactly the off-road areas a player would otherwise cross on generic
grass.
